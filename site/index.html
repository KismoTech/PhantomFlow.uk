Phantom mode

Every run leaves a phantom.
Beat it.

Lay this run over your best one, a team-mate's, or a pro line. PhantomFlow shows where you gained time, where you lost it, and why: the line, the braking, the speed you carried, or the bike.

  • Sector-by-sector delta against any phantom
  • Was the inside line faster on run 3 or run 5?
  • Did you carry enough speed to float the roots, or get hung up?
  • Change one thing, ride again, and see what it did

Engineering

Engineered for the worst day of the season.

Downhill is hard on electronics: mud, water, rock strikes and constant vibration. PhantomFlow is designed for that from the start.

  • Unaffected by mudNon-contact sensing. No lens to wipe, no slider to clog.
  • Fully sealedNo exposed moving parts. Designed for rain and the pressure washer.
  • Fits in secondsNo precise alignment, linkages or tools. Move it between bikes in the pits.
  • Built to take hitsSolid-state, for flat landings, rock strikes and constant vibration.
  • One picture of the runThe app combines the device with GPS, accelerometer, cadence and wheel-speed data, all on one timeline.
  • Never drops a runEvery run is stored on the bike, so the data survives when the signal drops in the woods.
